# Homeostasis concept inventory pretest and posttest scores

`HCIprepost` dataset consists of the pretest and posttest score of 16
students to Homeostasis Concept Inventory (HCI). Between the pre-test
and post-test, the students received instruction on homeostasis within a
physiology course.

## Usage

``` r
HCIprepost
```

## Format

`HCIprepost` is a `data.frame` consisting of 16 observations on the 2
variables.

- id:

  Anonymized respondent ID.

- score.pre:

  Pretest score.

- score.post:

  Posttest score.
